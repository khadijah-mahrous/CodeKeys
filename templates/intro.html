Before You Start Typing

  • ✓ Sit up straight: Good posture helps you type faster.
  • ✓ Wrists: Place them comfortably on the desk.
  • ✓ Home Row: Left fingers on A-S-D-F, Right on J-K-L-;
  • ✓ Thumbs: Should rest on the spacebar.
  • ⚠ Never look at the keyboard: The screen will guide you.
